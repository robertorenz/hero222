Design notes

  • Each screen is 20 × 12 tiles. Cyan lines mark screen edges; the view flips when the hero crosses one.
  • The hero respawns where they entered the screen, so keep entry points clear of lava.
  • Dynamite must be dropped while standing. Give each wall a floor beside it.
  • A blast clears the dynamite tile, two tiles in each direction, and the diagonals.
  • Saved caverns live in this browser. Use Export to share them.
A tribute to the 1984 Activision classic by John Van Ryzin. Built with plain HTML5 canvas — no plugins, nothing to install.
